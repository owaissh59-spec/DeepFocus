# 📘 COMPOUNDING, DISPENSING & PHARMACEUTICS
## A Complete Topper's Notes Book
### For Pharmacist / Compounder / Dispenser / Drug Inspector & allied competitive examinations

---

> **Scope of this book** — This book covers, exhaustively and line-by-line, the following
> syllabus block:
>
> 1. Kinds of drugs, characteristics of drugs, Balsams, Gums etc.
> 2. Pharmaceutical Process and Methods
> 3. General directions on dispensing, weighing and measuring. How prescriptions are written,
>    prescription reading. How to calculate doses, weights and measures, formulae for converting
>    from one scale to other, abbreviations used in prescriptions.
> 4. Doses of drugs, pharmacopoeial preparations and their doses, incompatibility — physical,
>    chemical, physiological and therapeutical.
> 5. **Suppositories** — How to prepare suppositories of special medicines, pessaries, bougies,
>    plasters etc.
> 6. Ointments, Spray solutions or Nebulas, Inhalations, General rules about preparation of
>    Mixtures.
> 7. Ordinary bazaar medicines, their recognition, doses and uses.

## 🎯 HOW TO USE THIS BOOK

1. **First pass (understanding).** Read a chapter fully, ignoring nothing. Every table in this
   book exists because that table's content has been asked as an objective question.
2. **Second pass (extraction).** Read only 📌 ⭐ 🔢 ⚠️ boxes and all tables.
3. **Third pass (24 h before exam).** Read only `14-appendices-and-revision.md`.
4. **Numericals** (Ch. 6, Ch. 9 displacement value) must be *worked with a pen*, not read.
5. **Latin abbreviations** (Ch. 5) and **bazaar medicines** (Ch. 13) are pure memory scoring
   areas — revise them daily in small doses rather than once in bulk.

## ⚖️ A NOTE ON DOSES & FORMULAE

* All doses quoted are **adult oral doses** unless otherwise stated.
* Doses and formulae follow the classical pharmacopoeial/dispensing literature
  (Indian Pharmacopoeia, British Pharmacopoeia, British Pharmaceutical Codex,
  *Cooper & Gunn's Dispensing for Pharmaceutical Students*, *Bentley's Textbook of
  Pharmaceutics*, *Carter's Cooper & Gunn's Tutorial Pharmacy*) because **that is what the
  examination is set from**.
* Several of these classical preparations are obsolete in modern therapeutics; they are retained
  here **only because the syllabus and question papers still test them**.
* Ranges differ by one or two units between editions. Where an examiner's key may differ, the
  book gives the **most commonly printed value first**, with alternatives in brackets.
* ⚠️ This is an **examination revision aid, not a prescribing guide.** Never dispense or
  administer on the strength of these notes alone.
