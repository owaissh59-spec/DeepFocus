# APPENDIX A — 🔢 MASTER CONVERSION & NUMBER SHEET

### A1. Metric
`1 kg = 1000 g · 1 g = 1000 mg · 1 mg = 1000 µg · 1 L = 1000 mL · 1 mL = 1 cc ·
1 mL water = 1 g · 1 m = 100 cm = 1000 mm`

### A2. Apothecary chains (memorise the two chains)
* **WEIGHT:** **20 grains = 1 scruple → 3 scruples (60 gr) = 1 drachm → 8 drachms (480 gr) =
  1 ounce → 12 ounces (5760 gr) = 1 pound (troy)**
* **VOLUME:** **60 minims = 1 fluid drachm → 8 fl drachms = 1 fluid ounce → 20 fl oz = 1 pint →
  8 pints = 1 gallon**
* **Avoirdupois:** 1 oz = 437.5 gr = 28.35 g; **16 oz = 1 lb = 7000 gr = 453.6 g**

### A4. Household measures
`1 teaspoonful = 5 mL · 1 dessertspoonful = 8 mL · 1 tablespoonful = 15 mL · 1 wineglassful = 60 mL ·
1 teacupful = 120 mL · 1 tumblerful = 240 mL · 1 drop ≈ 0.05 mL (20 drops = 1 mL)`

### A5. Temperature
`°F = (9/5 × °C) + 32 · °C = 5/9 × (°F − 32) · K = °C + 273 · SAME reading at −40°`
`Water: 0 °C = 32 °F; 100 °C = 212 °F · Body: 37 °C = 98.6 °F`

### A6. Percentage & ratio
`% w/w = g/100 g · % w/v = g/100 mL · % v/v = mL/100 mL · % v/w = mL/100 g`
`1 % w/v = 10 mg/mL · 1 : 1000 = 0.1 % · 1 ppm = 0.0001 % = 1 mg/L = 1 µg/mL`
`Ratio 1 : x → % = 100/x · Dilution: Q₁C₁ = Q₂C₂`

### A7. Specific gravities
`Water 1.00 · Alcohol 0.79 · Ether 0.72 · Glycerin 1.26 · Chloroform 1.48 ·
Syrup 1.32 · Liquid paraffin 0.83–0.89 · Castor oil 0.95 · Olive/arachis oil 0.91 ·
Clove oil 1.04–1.06 (heavier than water) · Mercury 13.6`
`Weight = Volume × Sp. gr. · Volume = Weight ÷ Sp. gr.`

### A8. Balance & error
`Class A: SR = 6 mg · capacity 120 g · MWQ = 120 mg · allowed error 5 %`
`Class B: SR = 30 mg · MWQ ≈ 600–650 mg`
`MWQ = (SR × 100) ÷ % error · % error = (error ÷ desired quantity) × 100`
`Aliquot: multiple factor = MWQ ÷ required quantity`

### A9. Dose formulae
`Young's = A × age/(age+12) · Dilling's = A × age/20 · Cowling's = A × (age next birthday)/24 ·
Fried's = A × months/150 · Clark's = A × lb/150 (or kg/68) · BSA = A × child BSA/1.73`
`Adult = 150 lb (68–70 kg), BSA 1.73 m² · Mosteller BSA = √(ht cm × wt kg ÷ 3600)`
`Volume to give = (dose required ÷ stock strength) × stock volume`
`Drops/min = (volume × drop factor) ÷ minutes (macro 15–20, micro 60 drops/mL)`

### A10. Isotonicity & mEq
`0.9 % NaCl · 5 % dextrose · 1.9 % boric acid = isotonic · Blood freezing point −0.52 °C ·
ΔTf of 1 % NaCl = 0.576 · Osmolarity 285–310 mOsm/L`
`NaCl to add = (0.9 × volume/100) − (weight of drug × E value) · White–Vincent: V = W × E × 111.1`
`1 mEq = mol. wt ÷ valency (mg): Na⁺ 23 · K⁺ 39 · Ca²⁺ 20 · Mg²⁺ 12 · Cl⁻ 35.5 · HCO₃⁻ 61 ·
NaCl 58.5 · KCl 74.5 · NaHCO₃ 84 · Normal saline ≈ 154 mEq/L`

### A11. Alcohol
`Proof spirit = 57.1 % v/v · Rectified spirit = 90 % · Absolute alcohol = 99.5 % (175.25° proof) ·
Degrees proof = (% v/v × 100) ÷ 57.1 · Antiseptic optimum = 70 %`

### A12. Storage temperatures
`Deep freezer −20 to −10 °C · Cold/refrigerator 2–8 °C · Cool 8–25 °C ·
Room temperature 15–30 °C · Warm 30–40 °C · Excessive heat > 40 °C`

### A13. Sterilisation numbers
`Autoclave 121 °C / 15 psi / 15 min (also 115 °C/10 psi/30 min; 134 °C/30 psi/3 min) ·
Hot air oven 160 °C / 2 h (170 °C/1 h; 180 °C/30 min) · Depyrogenation 250 °C / 30 min ·
Pasteurisation 63 °C/30 min or 72 °C/15 s · Tyndallisation 100 °C, 20–60 min × 3 days ·
Gamma 2.5 Mrad (25 kGy) Cobalt-60 · UV 254 nm · ETO 45–63 °C, 30–60 % RH ·
Membrane 0.22 µm · HEPA 99.97 % of 0.3 µm · F₀ ≥ 8–12 · SAL 10⁻⁶ · Z-value ≈ 10 °C`
`Biological indicators: B. stearothermophilus → autoclave · B. subtilis → dry heat & ETO ·
B. pumilus → radiation`

### A14. Particle sizes
`Sieve no. = apertures per linear inch (higher no. = finer)`
`IP powders: Coarse 10/44 · Mod. coarse 22/60 · Mod. fine 44/85 · Fine 85 · Very fine 120 ·
Microfine 90 % < 50 µm · Superfine 90 % < 10 µm`
`Inhalation: > 10 µm throat · 5–10 µm bronchi · 1–5 µm ALVEOLI (optimum) · < 1 µm exhaled`

# APPENDIX F — ⚡ THE 150 LAST-MINUTE ONE-LINERS

**DRUGS & SOURCES**
1. Drug ← French **"Drogue"** = dry herb.
2. Sources: plant, animal, mineral, microbial, marine, synthetic, semi-synthetic, biotech.
3. **Organised** drug has cells (microscopy); **unorganised** has none (physical/chemical tests).
4. Liquid & soft paraffin are **MINERAL**; wool fat, beeswax, cod liver oil are **ANIMAL**.
5. **Antiseptic** = living tissue; **disinfectant** = inanimate object.
6. **Mydriatic** dilates; **miotic** constricts.
7. **Vermifuge** expels; **vermicide** kills.
8. **Emollient** = skin; **demulcent** = mucosa.
9. **Expectorant** brings sputum out; **antitussive** suppresses cough.
10. Evaluation = **Organoleptic, Microscopic, Physical, Chemical, Biological**.
11. **Acid-insoluble ash** detects sand/earthy adulteration.
12. **Iodine value** = unsaturation; **acid value** = rancidity; **saponification value** ∝ 1/mol. wt;
    **acetyl value high** = castor oil.
13. **Isapghula swelling index ≥ 40**.
14. **Efflorescent**: Na₂CO₃·10H₂O, FeSO₄, MgSO₄, alum, citric acid.
15. **Deliquescent**: CaCl₂, ZnCl₂, NaOH, KOH, FeCl₃.
16. **Sublimable**: camphor, menthol, thymol, iodine, benzoic acid, NH₄Cl, naphthalene.
17. **Volatile oil** → temporary stain, steam-distillable; **fixed oil** → permanent stain, rancid.
18. IP **1955** · NFI **1960** · BP **1864** · USP **1820** · *International Pharmacopoeia* = **WHO
    (advisory only)**.
19. **Schedule H** prescription-only · **H1** red-box · **X** narcotic (copy 2 yr) · **G** medical
    supervision · **M** GMP · **N** pharmacy equipment · **P** shelf-life · **Y** clinical trials.
20. Only a **registered pharmacist** may dispense — **Pharmacy Act 1948, Sec. 42**.

**GUMS, RESINS & BALSAMS**
21. **Gum** = pathological, carbohydrate, water-soluble, alcohol-insoluble.
22. **Resin** = alcohol-soluble, water-insoluble, burns with a sooty flame.
23. **Balsam MUST contain benzoic/cinnamic acid** → only **Tolu, Peru, Storax, Benzoin**.
24. **Copaiba, Canada & Gurjun "balsams" are OLEORESINS.**
25. **Acacia** = *Acacia senegal*, Leguminosae, **arabin + OXIDASE**, best **emulgent**, mucilage 35 %.
26. **Tragacanth** = *Astragalus gummifer*, **bassorin + tragacanthin**, **swells**, best
    **suspending agent**, ruthenium red → pink.
27. **Karaya = Sterculia urens** (Sterculiaceae), swells 60–100×, bulk laxative.
28. **Guar gum = galactomannan**; **Agar** = red algae, "vegetable gelatin", gels 32–39 °C.
29. **Colophony** = residue after distilling volatile oil off *Pinus* oleoresin; **abietic acid**;
    adhesive plasters.
30. **Asafoetida** = *Ferula*, alliaceous (sulphur compounds), carminative, 0.3–1 g.
31. **Myrrh** = *Commiphora molmol*, Burseraceae, gargle.
32. **Male fern** → tapeworm, **filicin**, ⚠️ never with castor oil/alcohol/fat.
33. **Jalap** = jalapin + convolvulin; **Podophyllum** = warts & source of etoposide.
34. **Sumatra benzoin** = benzoic + cinnamic acid, coniferyl **cinnamate**, KMnO₄ **positive**;
    **Siam** = benzoic only, coniferyl **benzoate**, KMnO₄ negative.
35. **Peru balsam** = cinnamein, **insoluble in petroleum spirit**, scabies, from **El Salvador**.
36. **Tolu** = expectorant (Syrup of Tolu); **Storax** = *Liquidambar*, Hamamelidaceae.
37. **Opium** = *Papaver somniferum* latex, **NLT 9.5 % morphine**, **meconic acid** test.
38. **Shellac** = insect resin → **enteric coating**.
39. ⭐ **Castor oil = the only fixed oil freely soluble in alcohol.**
40. **Friar's Balsam = Benzoin 10, Storax 7.5, Tolu 2.5, Aloes 2.5.**

**PROCESSES**
41. **Rittinger** = fine grinding; **Kick** = coarse; **Bond** = between.
42. **Fluid energy mill** = micronising & thermolabile (no moving parts).
43. **Ball mill** runs at **50–80 % of critical speed**; can grind wet/dry & sterile.
44. **Hammer mill** = most versatile; **cutter mill** = fibrous crude drugs.
45. **Triple roller/ colloid mill** = ointments & emulsions.
46. Dry grinding < 5 % moisture; wet grinding > 50 %.
47. **Sieve no. = apertures per linear inch**; higher = finer.
48. Powders: **coarse 10/44, mod. coarse 22/60, mod. fine 44/85, fine 85, very fine 120**.
49. **Drying** → solid product; **evaporation** → liquid product.
50. **Spray dryer** = thermolabile liquids (seconds of contact); **freeze dryer** = sublimation of
    ice under vacuum (vaccines, sera); **FBD** = tablet granules; **vacuum dryer** = thermolabile
    solids.
51. **Steam distillation** → volatile oils; **molecular distillation** → vitamins A & E;
    **destructive distillation** → tar; alcohol–water azeotrope **95.6 %**.
52. **Menstruum** = solvent; **marc** = exhausted residue.
53. **Maceration** = static, 7 days, unorganised drugs; **percolation** = continuous, organised &
    potent drugs.
54. Percolation: **size reduction → imbibition → packing → maceration 24 h → percolation →
    adjustment**; rates **slow 1, moderate 1–3, rapid 3–5 mL/min**.
55. **Reserved percolation** protects heat-labile constituents (reserve ¾).
56. **Soxhlet** = continuous hot percolation.
57. Filtration rate ∝ **area × pressure ÷ (viscosity × cake thickness × resistance)**.
58. **Filter aid** (kieselguhr, talc) prevents clogging — never when the solid is the product.
59. **0.22 µm** = sterilising filter; **HEPA** = 99.97 % of 0.3 µm; **Meta filter** needs kieselguhr.
60. Rapid cooling + agitation → **small** crystals; slow cooling → **large**.
61. **Polymorphism** affects bioavailability (chloramphenicol palmitate form B is the active one).
62. **Autoclave 121 °C/15 psi/15 min** = coagulation of protein (moist heat).
63. **Hot air oven 160 °C/2 h** = oxidation (dry heat).
64. **Depyrogenation 250 °C/30 min**; pyrogens are **LPS of Gram-negative bacteria**, heat-stable,
    detected by the **rabbit & LAL tests**.
65. **Gamma 2.5 Mrad (Co-60)** for disposables; **UV 254 nm** is surface-only; **ETO** is an
    alkylating agent needing moisture.
66. Sterility test media: **Fluid Thioglycollate (30–35 °C)** & **Soyabean Casein Digest
    (20–25 °C)**, 14 days.
67. **Exsiccation** = removing water of crystallisation; **calcination** = strong heating in air;
    **torrefaction** = roasting; **lixiviation** = leaching; **fusion** = melting together.

**DISPENSING**
68. Dispensing = prepare + pack + label + supply; compounding is only one step.
69. **Three-check rule** — read the label from the shelf, at use, and on return.
70. **5 Rights:** patient, drug, dose, route/form, time.
71. **Wedgwood mortar** = grinding hard crystals; **glass mortar** = staining/corrosive substances;
    **horn/plastic spatula** = iodine, tannins, mercurials, silver salts.
72. **Class A balance: SR 6 mg, capacity 120 g, MWQ 120 mg, error 5 %.**
73. **MWQ = (SR × 100) ÷ % error.**
74. Weights on the **left** pan, substance on the **right**; handle weights with **forceps**.
75. **Cylindrical measure is more accurate; conical is more convenient** (viscous liquids).
76. Never use a measure for **< one-fifth** of its capacity.
77. Read the **bottom of the meniscus at eye level** (mercury: the top).
78. **WEIGH viscous liquids** (glycerin, paraffin, syrup); **MEASURE volatile liquids** quickly.
79. **Hermetically sealed** = impervious to gases, cannot be reopened (ampoule).
80. **Glass: Type I borosilicate = all parenterals · II treated soda-lime = acidic aqueous ·
    III = oily & powders · IV (NP) = oral/topical only.**
81. ⭐ **RIBBED bottle + RED label + "FOR EXTERNAL USE ONLY"** for liniments/lotions.
82. **"Shake well"** on suspensions & emulsions — **never** on a true solution.
83. **Eye drops: discard 4 weeks after opening; eye lotion (collyrium): 24 hours,
    preservative-free.**
84. Always **0.5 mL** (leading zero), never **5.0 mL** (no trailing zero).

**PRESCRIPTION**
85. **Seven parts:** date · patient particulars · superscription (℞) · inscription · subscription ·
    signatura · signature/refill.
86. **℞ = "Recipe" = take thou** (also a dedication to **Jupiter**).
87. **Inscription = Basis + Adjuvant + Corrective + Vehicle.**
88. **Subscription = to the PHARMACIST; Signatura = to the PATIENT.**
89. Handling: **receive → read → label → collect/number → compound → pack → recheck & deliver**.
90. ⚠️ **Never guess; never alter; contact the prescriber and record the change.**
91. **Schedule X: duplicate, quantity in words, copy kept 2 years, never repeated.**
92. **a.c. before food · p.c. after food · t.d.s. thrice · q.d.s. four times · h.s. bedtime ·
    p.r.n. when required · s.o.s. if necessary · stat. at once.**
93. **a.d. right EAR · o.d. right EYE (in eye Rx) · gutt. drops · ad part. dolent. to the painful
    part.**
94. **gr = GRAIN (65 mg); g = GRAM.**

**DOSES & PREPARATIONS**
95. Dose ladder: **minimum → therapeutic → maximum → toxic → lethal**.
96. **Therapeutic index = LD₅₀/ED₅₀**; higher = safer; narrow-TI: **digoxin, lithium, warfarin,
    phenytoin, theophylline, aminoglycosides**.
97. **Parenteral dose < oral dose** (first-pass & incomplete absorption).
98. **Cumulation** = digitalis, emetine, heavy metals, bromides; **tolerance** = morphine,
    barbiturates, nitrates.
99. **Enzyme inducers**: phenobarbitone, phenytoin, **rifampicin**, carbamazepine, griseofulvin.
100. **Enzyme inhibitors**: cimetidine, erythromycin, ketoconazole, metronidazole, INH.
101. **Tincture 1 in 5 / 1 in 10 · Liquid extract 1 in 1 · Infusion 1 in 10 (12 h) ·
     Concentrated infusion 8× (1+7).**
102. **Paregoric (camphorated tincture of opium) 2–8 mL; Laudanum (tincture of opium) 0.3–2 mL.**
103. **Dover's powder = Ipecac 10 + Opium 10 + Lactose 80; dose 300–600 mg.**
104. **Magnesium sulphate 5–15 g · Castor oil 5–20 mL · Liquid paraffin 10–30 mL ·
     Isapghula 4–16 g · Ferrous sulphate 200 mg t.d.s. · Paracetamol 500 mg–1 g (max 4 g/day) ·
     Atropine 0.6 mg · Adrenaline 0.5 mL of 1:1000 IM · Activated charcoal 50–100 g.**
105. **LVP must be isotonic, pyrogen-free and preservative-FREE.**
106. **Potent drugs are dispensed as DIVIDED powders.**

**INCOMPATIBILITY**
107. **Physical** = no reaction; **chemical** = new substance; **therapeutic** = altered effect in
     the body.
108. **Eutectic** = melts lower than either component: **camphor, menthol, thymol, phenol,
     chloral hydrate, betanaphthol, resorcinol, salol, aspirin, phenacetin**.
109. Correct a eutectic by **spatulation + an ABSORBENT (light MgCO₃, light kaolin, starch)**.
110. Efflorescent salts in powders → use the **EXSICCATED form with the weight adjusted**.
111. **Adjusted (tolerated)** = the pharmacist may correct it; **unadjusted** = refer to the
     prescriber.
112. **Alkaloid + alkali → free base precipitates; alkaloid + tannin → insoluble tannate;
     salicylate/phenol + Fe³⁺ → violet; carbonate + acid → effervescence.**
113. ⚠️ **Permanganate/chlorate + glycerin/sugar/charcoal/sulphur → EXPLOSION; never triturate.**
114. **Aspirin + moisture/alkali → hydrolysis (vinegar smell).**
115. **Acacia's oxidase** destroys oxidisable drugs → heat the mucilage 100 °C/30 min.
116. Therapeutic classics: **morphine + strychnine · tetracycline + milk/antacid/iron ·
     liquid paraffin + vitamins A D E K · male fern + castor oil · sulphonamide + procaine (PABA) ·
     rifampicin + oral contraceptive · levodopa + pyridoxine.**
117. **Universal antidote = charcoal 2 : MgO 1 : tannic acid 1.**
118. **IV: nothing is added to blood; amphotericin B only in 5 % dextrose;
     calcium + bicarbonate → precipitate; penicillin + aminoglycoside → inactivation.**

**SUPPOSITORIES & PLASTERS**
119. **Suppository = rectum (1 g) · Pessary = vagina (4–5 g) · Bougie = urethra (male 4 g ×
     100–150 mm, female 2 g × 60–75 mm).**
120. Insert a suppository **blunt end first**.
121. Rectal route **partly avoids first-pass metabolism** (lower & middle rectal veins → IVC).
122. **Theobroma oil** = *Theobroma cacao* (Sterculiaceae), m.p. **30–35 °C**;
     **polymorphs γ 18, α 22–24, β′ 28, β (stable) 34–35 °C**; ⭐ **never heat above 36 °C —
     melt only two-thirds**.
123. **Glycero-gelatin = Gelatin 14 + Glycerin 70 + Water 16**; hygroscopic, needs a preservative,
     **moisten before insertion**, **mould must be lubricated**.
124. **PEG bases** need no refrigeration but **sting** and are **incompatible with benzocaine,
     aspirin, phenol, sulphonamides, silver salts & plastics**.
125. **Witepsol/ hydrogenated bases**: sharp m.p., no polymorphism, no rancidity, but **brittle on
     thermal shock**.
126. **Beeswax/white wax raises the melting point** (chloral hydrate, phenol, camphor, hot climates).
127. **DV: ZnO 4.7 · bismuth subgallate 2.7 · aspirin 1.1 · ichthammol 1.0 · camphor & menthol 0.7 ·
     default 1.5.**
128. **Base = (n × mould size) − (total drug ÷ DV)**; **make 2 extra**.
129. **Calibrate the mould** because it measures **volume**, not weight.
130. **Overfill the mould** (the mass contracts) and **pour while thickening with constant stirring**.
131. **Lubricant must be IMMISCIBLE with the base** — aqueous for fatty bases, liquid paraffin for
     water-soluble bases.
132. Special medicines: **soluble → dissolve · insoluble → levigate · water-soluble → minimum water
     + wool fat, or powder it · volatile/thermolabile → last & coolest.**
133. **Disintegration: fatty base 30 min, water-soluble base 60 min.**
134. **Lead (Diachylon) plaster = lead oxide + olive oil + water → lead oleo-palmitate.**
135. **Salicylic acid plaster 10–40 % = keratolytic (corns & warts).**
136. ⭐ **Mustard plaster must be moistened with LUKEWARM water — hot water destroys MYROSIN.**
137. **Poultice is applied AS HOT AS BEARABLE to retain heat** (kaolin, linseed).

**OINTMENTS & MIXTURES & SPRAYS**
138. **Ointment = greasy · Cream = emulsion (water) · Paste = 20–50 % powder, stiff, stays put ·
     Gel = water-soluble & non-greasy.**
139. **4 bases: oleaginous (most occlusive, best absorption) · absorption (wool fat, takes up
     water) · emulsifying o/w (washable, needs preservative) · water-soluble PEG (greaseless,
     hygroscopic, many incompatibilities).**
140. **Emulsifying wax = anionic (SLS) · Cetrimide wax = cationic · Cetomacrogol wax = non-ionic
     (compatible with both).**
141. **Fusion: melt in DESCENDING order of melting point and stir until COLD.**
142. **Levigating agent must be MISCIBLE with the base** (liquid paraffin for greasy, glycerin/water
     for water-miscible).
143. **Creams: heat both phases separately to the SAME temperature (60–70 °C).**
144. **o/w emulsifier HLB 8–18; w/o HLB 3–6; o/w conducts electricity.**
145. **DIFFUSIBLE: magnesium carbonate/oxide/trisilicate, chalk, LIGHT kaolin, bismuth salts.
     INDIFFUSIBLE: aspirin, sulphur, calamine, zinc oxide, phenobarbitone, HEAVY kaolin.**
146. **Suspending agent = compound tragacanth powder 2 %; it works by raising VISCOSITY
     (Stokes' law).**
147. **Flocculated = fluffy, re-disperses, no caking (good); deflocculated = hard cake (bad).**
148. **Mixture rules: soluble solids → liquids → suspending agent mixed DRY then levigated →
     tinctures added SLOWLY to the BULK of the vehicle → make up to volume with the rinsings →
     "Shake well".**
149. **Chloroform water 0.25 % is the vehicle & preservative;** emulsion ratios **4:2:1 / 3:2:1 /
     2:2:1 / 1:2:1**; the primary emulsion is ready at the **"crackling" sound**.
150. **Inhalation: 5 mL (1 mL benzoin tincture) in 500 mL water at 65 °C — NOT boiling
     (scalding, rapid loss, resin coagulates); light magnesium carbonate is the DISPERSING agent;
     1–5 µm particles reach the alveoli; CFCs are banned (ozone) → HFA-134a;
     oily nasal drops cause LIPOID PNEUMONIA & ciliary paralysis.**
