⭐ **THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT DEFINITION TABLE OF THIS CHAPTER**

| Class | Composition | Key identifying feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Gum** | Carbohydrate (polysaccharide salts) | **Water-soluble/ swells**; alcohol-insoluble |
| **Mucilage** | Carbohydrate — **normal cell constituent** | Formed **inside** the plant, not by injury |
| **Resin** | Complex mixture of resin acids, resin alcohols (resinols), resinotannols, esters, inert resenes | Water-**insoluble**, **alcohol-soluble**; burns with sooty flame |
| **Oleoresin** | **Resin + Volatile oil** | Natural (Turpentine, Copaiba) or prepared (Ginger, Capsicum) |
| **Oleo-gum-resin** | **Resin + Volatile oil + Gum** | Partly water-, partly alcohol-soluble → **emulsion** with water |
| **Balsam** | **Resin + Benzoic acid and/or Cinnamic acid** (or their esters) | ⭐ **MUST contain benzoic/cinnamic acid** |
| **Glycoresin (glucoresin)** | Resin + glycoside | Jalap, Podophyllum, Ipomoea |
| **Latex** | Milky emulsion in latex vessels | Coagulates on exposure; e.g. Opium, Papain, Rubber |

🧠 **MEMORY HOOK — the balsam rule:**
> **"NO BENZOIC OR CINNAMIC ACID → NOT A BALSAM."**
> Therefore **Copaiba balsam, Canada balsam and Gurjun balsam are NOT true balsams** — they are
> **oleoresins**, wrongly named "balsam". ⚠️ *This is the single most-asked trap of the chapter.*

⭐ **TRUE BALSAMS (only these four are true):**
**Tolu balsam, Peru balsam, Storax (Styrax), Benzoin.**
🧠 "**T**he **P**harmacist **S**ells **B**alsams" → **T**olu, **P**eru, **S**torax, **B**enzoin.

⚠️ **FALSE BALSAMS (oleoresins in disguise):** **Copaiba, Canada balsam, Gurjun balsam,
Balsam of Mecca (partly), Japanese balsam.**

## 2.2 GUMS & MUCILAGES

### 2.2.1 Definitions & general nature

📌 **GUM** — A gum is a **pathological product** formed by the **gummosis** (disintegration of
cellulose/ starch of cell walls) of plant tissue as a result of **injury, insect attack or
unfavourable conditions**. Chemically, gums are **salts (Ca, Mg, K) of complex polysaccharide
acids** (uronic acids + sugars).

📌 **MUCILAGE** — A mucilage is a **normal physiological product** of metabolism, formed **within
the cell** (in mucilage cells, canals or cavities) **without injury**; chemically it is also a
polysaccharide, and it forms slimy masses with water.

📌 **In pharmacy**, "mucilage" also means the *viscous aqueous solution prepared from a gum* —
e.g. **Mucilage of Acacia (Mucilago Acaciae) = 35 % w/v** of acacia in water.

🔁 **COMPARE — GUM vs MUCILAGE**

| Feature | GUM | MUCILAGE |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | **Pathological** (injury/disease) | **Physiological** (normal metabolism) |
| Site of formation | Exudes **outside** the plant | Formed & stored **inside** the cell |
| Water | Dissolves or swells → **adhesive/ sticky** | Forms **slimy** mass |
| Alcohol | Insoluble | Insoluble |
| Adhesiveness | **Strongly adhesive** | Weakly adhesive |
| Examples | Acacia, Tragacanth, Karaya, Ghatti | Isapghula, Linseed, Agar, Salep, Aloe leaf gel |

🔁 **COMPARE — GUM vs RESIN** (asked in almost every paper)

| Feature | GUM | RESIN |
|---|---|---|
| Chemistry | Carbohydrate (polysaccharide) | Non-carbohydrate; resin acids/alcohols/esters, terpenoid or phenolic |
| Nitrogen | Absent | Absent |
| Solubility in **water** | **Soluble / swells** | **Insoluble** |
| Solubility in **alcohol** | **Insoluble** | **Soluble** |
| Solubility in organic solvents (ether, chloroform, acetone) | Insoluble | Soluble |
| On heating | Chars, smells of burnt sugar (caramel) | **Softens & melts**, burns with a **luminous sooty flame** |
| Hydrolysis | Gives sugars & uronic acids | Does not give sugar (except glycoresins) |
| Use | Suspending, emulsifying, binding, demulcent | Antiseptic, purgative, plaster/varnish, adhesive |

### 2.2.2 Uses of gums & mucilages in pharmacy

⭐ **The 8 pharmaceutical uses (very frequently asked):**
1. **Suspending agent** (indiffusible powders in mixtures) — Tragacanth, Acacia, Sterculia.
2. **Emulsifying agent** — Acacia (the classic **primary emulsifier**), Tragacanth (thickener).
3. **Binder/ granulating agent** in tablets — Acacia (10–25 %), Starch mucilage (5–10 %),
   Tragacanth.
4. **Demulcent & soothing agent** (cough linctuses, throat pastilles) — Acacia, Tragacanth, Honey.
5. **Bulk laxative** — Isapghula, Sterculia, Agar, Guar gum, Linseed.
6. **Adhesive** — mucilage of acacia/ tragacanth for labels, pill excipient.
7. **Thickening/ stabilising agent** in creams, jellies, toothpaste.
8. **Gelling/ suspending in food, and colloid stabiliser**; also **plasma substitute**
   (dextran, acacia historically), **tablet coating**, and **culture medium base (Agar)**.

### 2.2.3 🧪 GENERAL IDENTIFICATION TESTS FOR GUMS

| Test | Procedure | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Solubility** | Water vs alcohol | Soluble/swells in water, insoluble in alcohol (90 %) |
| **Ruthenium red** | Mount powder in ruthenium red | **Pink/red** → mucilage present |
| **Iodine** | Add iodine solution | **No blue** (differentiates from starch) |
| **Lead acetate** | Add to solution | Precipitate |
| **Molisch's test** | α-naphthol + conc. H₂SO₄ | Purple/violet ring (carbohydrate) |
| **Fehling's after hydrolysis** | Hydrolyse then Fehling's | Brick-red ppt (reducing sugars) |
| **Charring** | Heat strongly | Smell of **burnt sugar / caramel** |

⭐ **THE FOUR CLASSICAL DIFFERENTIATING TESTS (Acacia vs Tragacanth) — memorise verbatim**

| Test | ACACIA | TRAGACANTH |
|---|---|---|
| **Water** | **Completely & freely soluble** → clear, non-viscous, slightly acidic mucilage | **Insoluble but swells** → stiff, opalescent, highly viscous gel |
| **Ruthenium red** | Not stained/faint | **Stains pink** |
| **Iodine** (with the gum) | No colour | **Olive-green** with a small amount of starch present (tragacanth contains starch) |
| **Lead acetate (basic/ neutral)** | Precipitate with **lead acetate** | Precipitate with **basic lead acetate** |
| **Peroxidase / oxidase (H₂O₂ + benzidine)** | ⭐ **POSITIVE — deep blue** (acacia contains **oxidase/peroxidase enzyme**) | **NEGATIVE** |
| **FeCl₃** | No change | No change |
| **Viscosity** | Low | **Very high (most viscous of all gums)** |

🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**A**cacia has the **enzym A**se (oxidase → blue with benzidine + H₂O₂);
**T**ragacanth is **T**hick and **T**urns ruthenium red pink."

⚠️ **TRAP** — Because acacia contains **oxidase (peroxidase) enzyme**, it **oxidises easily
oxidisable drugs** (e.g. adrenaline, apomorphine, thymol, phenol, tannins, alkaloid salts,
easily oxidised vitamins). To destroy the enzyme, **heat the mucilage at 100 °C for ~30 min**
(or use another emulgent).

⭐ **Emulsifying capacity ("Gum : Oil : Water" ratios) — the FOUR classic ratios**

| Type of oil | Oil : Water : Gum (Acacia) |
|---|---|
| **Fixed oil** (e.g. castor, arachis, olive, cod-liver) | **4 : 2 : 1** |
| **Mineral oil** (liquid paraffin) | **3 : 2 : 1** |
| **Volatile oil** (turpentine, peppermint, cinnamon) | **2 : 2 : 1** |
| **Oleo-resins** (e.g. balsam of Peru, male fern) | **1 : 2 : 1** |

🧠 **MEMORY HOOK — "4-3-2-1 for Fixed, Mineral, Volatile, Oleoresin."** (Water & gum stay 2 : 1.)
⭐ **Dry gum method = Continental method (4:2:1)**; **Wet gum method = English method**;
**Bottle/ Forbes bottle method** for volatile oils; **Nascent soap method** for oil + lime water
(*in situ* soap = calcium oleate, e.g. **Lime liniment/ Carron oil = olive oil + lime water 1:1**).

## 2.3 RESINS

### 2.3.1 Definition & general properties

📌 **DEFINITION** — **Resins** are **amorphous, solid or semi-solid, non-nitrogenous,
non-crystalline complex mixtures** of resin acids, resin alcohols (resinols), resino-tannols,
esters and inert resenes; they are **insoluble in water**, **soluble in alcohol, ether, chloroform
and other organic solvents**, and are formed as a result of **oxidation and polymerisation of
volatile oils / terpenes** in the plant. They soften and melt on heating and burn with a
**characteristic sooty, luminous flame**.

### 2.3.2 Classification of resins

**(A) By composition**

| Class | Composition | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Resins (true/ pure)** | Resin only | **Colophony (rosin), Mastic, Sandarac, Guaiacum, Cannabis (charas)** |
| **Oleoresins** | Resin + volatile oil | **Turpentine, Copaiba, Canada balsam, Ginger, Capsicum, Male fern, Aspidium, Cubeb** |
| **Oleo-gum-resins** | Resin + volatile oil + gum | **Asafoetida, Myrrh, Gamboge, Ammoniacum, Galbanum, Olibanum (frankincense)** |
| **Balsams** | Resin + benzoic/ cinnamic acid | **Tolu, Peru, Storax, Benzoin** |
| **Glycoresins (glucoresins)** | Resin + glycoside | **Jalap, Podophyllum, Ipomoea (Indian jalap)** |

**(B) By chemical nature of constituents**

| Type | Chief constituent | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Resin acids** | Free acids (–COOH) — soluble in alkali, form soaps | Colophony (**abietic acid**), Copaiba (copaivic acid), Myrrh (commiphoric acid), Sandarac (sandaracopimaric acid) |
| **Resin esters** | Esters of resin acids/alcohols with benzoic, cinnamic, salicylic acid | **Benzoin, Storax, Peru, Tolu, Turpentine** |
| **Resin alcohols (resinols)** | High-molecular alcohols, no tannin reaction | **Benzoresinol, storesinol, guaiaresinol, peruresinol** (Benzoin, Storax) |
| **Resinotannols** | Resin alcohols giving **tannin reactions** (FeCl₃) | **Siaresinotannol, sumaresinotannol (Benzoin), peruresinotannol, toluresinotannol, aloeresinotannol, myroxocarpin** |
| **Resenes** | **Chemically inert**, neutral, no acid/alcohol/ester groups | **Colophorene** (colophony), **olibanoresene**, **gurjuresene**, **copaivic resene**, **cannabene** |
| **Glycoresins** | Resin combined with sugar | Jalapin/ convolvulin (Jalap), Podophyllotoxin glycoside (Podophyllum) |

🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Resin acid** = sour; **Resinol** = alcohol; **Resinotannol** = alcohol +
tannin (gives colour with FeCl₃); **Resene** = "**rest** — does nothing" (inert)."

### 2.3.3 🧪 GENERAL TESTS FOR RESINS

| Test | Observation |
|---|---|
| Solubility | Insoluble in water; **soluble in alcohol**, ether, chloroform, acetone, benzene |
| Alkali | Resin acids **dissolve in NaOH/KOH** forming soap-like solution |
| Heating | Softens, melts, then burns with **luminous, sooty (smoky) flame** and aromatic fumes |
| Alcoholic solution + water | **Turbidity/ milkiness** (resin precipitates) |
| FeCl₃ | Green/blue-black if **resinotannol** present (Benzoin, Peru, Tolu) |
| Litmus | Alcoholic solution of resin acids is **acidic** |

## 2.4 BALSAMS ⭐ (a "must-master" section — the syllabus names them explicitly)

### 2.4.1 Definition

📌 **DEFINITION** — **Balsams** are **resinous mixtures** that contain **large proportions of
benzoic acid, cinnamic acid, or their esters** (i.e. cinnamates/ benzoates).
They are **not** simply resin + volatile oil; the **presence of benzoic and/or cinnamic acid is
essential**.

⭐ **THE FOUR TRUE BALSAMS**

| Balsam | Contains |
|---|---|
| **Tolu balsam** | Benzoic + cinnamic acid (free & combined) |
| **Peru balsam** | Cinnamein (benzyl benzoate + benzyl cinnamate) |
| **Storax (Styrax)** | Cinnamic acid & its esters |
| **Benzoin** | Benzoic acid (Siam) / benzoic + cinnamic acid (Sumatra) |

⚠️ **NOT balsams (they lack benzoic/cinnamic acid) → they are OLEORESINS:**
**Copaiba balsam, Canada balsam, Gurjun balsam, Japan balsam, Mecca balsam.**

### 2.4.2 📋 MASTER MONOGRAPH TABLE — BALSAMS

| Drug | Synonyms | Biological source | Family | Chief constituents | Uses | Dose / Official preparations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BENZOIN** | **Gum benjamin, Loban, Benzoinum**; *Sumatra benzoin* & *Siam benzoin* | Balsamic resin obtained by incision of the stem of ***Styrax benzoin*** (Sumatra) and ***Styrax tonkinensis*** (Siam) | **Styracaceae** | **Sumatra:** free **benzoic + cinnamic acid**, coniferyl cinnamate, **sumaresinolic acid**, siaresinolic acid, phenylpropyl cinnamate, **vanillin**, styrol, styracin. **Siam:** ~ **benzoic acid only** (no cinnamic acid), **coniferyl benzoate (65–75 %), siaresinolic acid (19-hydroxy-oleanolic acid), vanillin** | ⭐ **Antiseptic, expectorant, protective (as tincture "compound tincture of benzoin"), carminative; inhalation for laryngitis & bronchitis; preservative for fats (antioxidant); incense; ingredient of Podophyllum paint** | **Compound Tincture of Benzoin (Friar's Balsam)** — inhalation **1 mL to 500 mL (1 pint) of hot water**; Benzoin 0.5–2 g; **Benzoic acid ointment (Whitfield's)** |
| **TOLU BALSAM** | Balsamum tolutanum, Thomas balsam | Balsam obtained from incised trunk of ***Myroxylon balsamum*** (var. *balsamum*) — Tolu, Colombia | **Leguminosae** | **Resin 75–80 % (toluresinotannol esters of benzoic & cinnamic acid)**, **free cinnamic acid (~ 12–15 %)** & **benzoic acid (~ 8 %)**, benzyl benzoate, **vanillin**, volatile oil traces | ⭐ **Expectorant & flavouring (Syrup of Tolu), mild antiseptic, ingredient of compound benzoin tincture; used in cough syrups & linctuses** | **Syrup of Tolu**; Tincture of Tolu **2–4 mL**; Balsam 0.5–2 g |

Benzoin Tincture; topical 10–20 % |

### 2.4.3 ⭐ COMPOUND TINCTURE OF BENZOIN — the exam's favourite formula

📌 **Compound Tincture of Benzoin B.P.** ("**Friar's Balsam**", "Jerusalem balsam",
"**Turlington's Balsam of Life**"):

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| **Benzoin (in coarse powder)** | **10 g** |
| **Prepared Storax** | **7.5 g** |
| **Tolu balsam** | **2.5 g** |
| **Aloes (in powder)** | **2.5 g** |
| **Alcohol (90 %)** | to **100 mL** |

🧠 **MEMORY HOOK — "10 – 7.5 – 2.5 – 2.5"** → **B**enzoin, **S**torax, **T**olu, **A**loes
("**B**ig **S**tar **T**akes **A**ll").
⭐ **Use:** as an **inhalation — 1 teaspoonful (about 1 mL, or 5 mL in some texts) added to
500 mL (1 pint) of hot (not boiling) water**, vapour inhaled; also a **protective & antiseptic
application to cracked skin**.
⚠️ Contains **Aloes** — a point often asked ("Which balsamic preparation contains aloes?").

### 2.4.4 🧪 IDENTIFICATION TESTS FOR BALSAMS ⭐⭐

| Test | Procedure | Result / Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Test for benzoic/ cinnamic acid (general balsam test)** | Sublimation: heat a little balsam on a slide | **Needle-like crystals** of benzoic/ cinnamic acid sublime |
| **Potassium permanganate test** | Balsam + KMnO₄ → warm | Odour of **benzaldehyde (bitter almonds)** → **cinnamic acid present** (Peru, Storax, Sumatra benzoin) |
| **FeCl₃ test** | Alcoholic extract + FeCl₃ | Green colour → **resinotannol** present |
| **Peru balsam — solubility test** | Shake with **petroleum spirit/light petroleum** | ⭐ **Peru balsam is INSOLUBLE** (Copaiba & other oleoresins dissolve) |
| **Peru balsam — Alcoholic KOH/ methanolic KOH** | Balsam + alcoholic KOH & warm | Clear solution; addition of water → precipitate |
| **Peru balsam — Cinnamein layer** | Shake with ether, evaporate | Oily "cinnamein" residue, pleasant vanilla-like odour |
| **Tolu balsam** | + Sulphuric acid & alcohol | **Red colour**; solution in ether + KOH → crystals of potassium cinnamate |
| **Benzoin (Siam vs Sumatra)** | Add KMnO₄ | **Sumatra gives benzaldehyde odour** (cinnamic acid present); **Siam does NOT** (only benzoic acid) |
| **Benzoin + Ether + ammonia (or NaOH)** | — | Sumatra benzoin gives red-violet with H₂SO₄–formaldehyde |
| **Storax** | Cinnamic acid detected by KMnO₄ → benzaldehyde | Positive |
| **Gum absent test** | Add water | True balsams give **no mucilage** (unlike oleo-gum-resins) |

🧠 **MEMORY HOOK — "SUMATRA = SUM of both acids (benzoic + cinnamic); SIAM = Single acid
(benzoic only)."**

🔁 **COMPARE — SIAM vs SUMATRA BENZOIN** (frequent question)

| Feature | **SIAM Benzoin** | **SUMATRA Benzoin** |
|---|---|---|
| Botanical source | ***Styrax tonkinensis*** | ***Styrax benzoin*** |
| Acid present | **Benzoic acid only** | **Benzoic + Cinnamic acid** |
| Chief ester | **Coniferyl benzoate (65–75 %)** | Coniferyl cinnamate |
| Triterpene acid | **Siaresinolic acid** | **Sumaresinolic acid** |
| KMnO₄ test (benzaldehyde odour) | **Negative** | **Positive** |
| Form | Compressed pebble-like tears, brittle, yellow-brown | Reddish-brown to greyish-brown block with **whitish tears** |
| Odour | Vanilla-like (contains **vanillin**) | Balsamic, storax-like |
| Official/ preferred | Preferred for **internal & pharmaceutical** use | Used mainly in **inhalations/ external & perfumery** |

🔁 **COMPARE — TOLU vs PERU BALSAM**

| Feature | **TOLU BALSAM** | **PERU BALSAM** |
|---|---|---|
| Source | *Myroxylon balsamum* var. *balsamum* | *Myroxylon balsamum* var. *pereirae* |
| Geographical source | Colombia/Venezuela (Tolu) | **El Salvador** (shipped via Peru) |
| Method of collection | V-shaped incisions in trunk | **Bark scorched & beaten**, rags soaked, then boiled |
| Consistency | **Brown, brittle solid → soft plastic solid** | **Dark brown, viscous liquid** |
| Chief constituent | **Free cinnamic & benzoic acids + resin esters** | **Cinnamein (benzyl benzoate + benzyl cinnamate)** |
| Odour | Aromatic, vanilla-like | Balsamic, vanilla-like |
| Petroleum spirit | Partly soluble | **Insoluble** ⭐ |
| Chief use | **Expectorant & flavour (Syrup of Tolu)** | **Parasiticide & wound healing (scabies)** |

## 2.7 CHAPTER 2 — RAPID REVISION SHEET

* **Gum** = pathological, carbohydrate, water-soluble, alcohol-insoluble.
  **Resin** = alcohol-soluble, water-insoluble, burns with sooty flame.
* **Balsam MUST contain benzoic and/or cinnamic acid** → only **Tolu, Peru, Storax, Benzoin**.
  Copaiba/ Canada/ Gurjun = **oleoresins**.
* **Acacia** → *Acacia senegal*, Leguminosae; contains **arabin + oxidase enzyme**;
  positive **benzidine/H₂O₂ test**; best **emulsifying agent**; mucilage **35 % w/v**.
* **Tragacanth** → *Astragalus gummifer*, Leguminosae; **bassorin + tragacanthin**;
  **swells, most viscous**; best **suspending agent**; stains **pink with ruthenium red**.
* **Karaya = Sterculia urens (Sterculiaceae)**, acetylated, swells 60–100×, bulk laxative,
  denture fixative.
* **Guar gum = galactomannan** from *Cyamopsis tetragonolobus*.
* **Agar** = red algae, "vegetable gelatin/ Japanese isinglass"; culture media; gels 32–39 °C.
* **Isapghula = Plantago ovata seeds**, mucilage in seed coat, **swelling index ≥ 40**, bulk
  laxative, 4–16 g.
* **Emulsion ratios: Fixed 4:2:1, Mineral 3:2:1, Volatile 2:2:1, Oleoresin 1:2:1.**
* **Compound Tragacanth Powder = Tragacanth 15 + Acacia 20 + Starch 20 + Sucrose 45.**
* **Colophony** = residue after distilling volatile oil from *Pinus* oleoresin; **abietic acid**;
  used in **adhesive plasters**.
* **Asafoetida (Hing)** = oleo-gum-resin of *Ferula* spp., **alliaceous odour due to sulphur
  compounds**, **carminative & antispasmodic**, dose **0.3–1 g**.
* **Myrrh** = *Commiphora molmol*, Burseraceae; gargle & mouthwash.
* **Male fern** = tapeworm; **filicin**; never with **castor oil/ alcohol/ fat**.
* **Jalap** = *Ipomoea purga* → **jalapin & convolvulin**, drastic purgative.
* **Podophyllum** = warts (paint 15–25 % in comp. benzoin tincture) & source of **etoposide**.
* **Compound Tincture of Benzoin (Friar's Balsam) = Benzoin 10, Storax 7.5, Tolu 2.5, Aloes 2.5,
  alcohol to 100 mL**; inhalation **1 mL in 500 mL hot water**.
* **Sumatra benzoin** → *Styrax benzoin*, benzoic **+ cinnamic** acid, coniferyl **cinnamate**,
  **sumaresinolic acid**, KMnO₄ **positive**.
  **Siam benzoin** → *Styrax tonkinensis*, **benzoic acid only**, coniferyl **benzoate**,
  **siaresinolic acid**, KMnO₄ **negative**.
* **Peru balsam** → cinnamein; **insoluble in petroleum spirit**; scabies & wound healing;
  from **El Salvador**.
* **Tolu balsam** → expectorant, **Syrup of Tolu**.
* **Storax** → *Liquidambar orientalis*, **Hamamelidaceae**, cinnamic acid + **styracin**.
* **Opium** = latex of *Papaver somniferum* capsule; **NLT 9.5 % morphine**;
  **meconic acid** identifies it; Paregoric = camphorated tincture of opium.
* **Shellac** = insect (*Laccifer lacca*) resin → **enteric coating**.
* **Castor oil = only fixed oil freely soluble in alcohol.**
